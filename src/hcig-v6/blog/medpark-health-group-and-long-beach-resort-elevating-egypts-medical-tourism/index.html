MedPark Health Group & Long Beach Resort: Elevating Egypt’s Medical Tourism

Hurghada, Egypt. MedPark Health Group had the privilege of welcoming the Executive Chairman of the Egyptian Arab Land Bank (EALB) and members of the Board, together with the Managing Director of El Akary for Touristic Development (EDIT) and the General Manager of Long Beach Resort, to our Hub in Hurghada, Sahl Hasheesh.

Their visit highlighted our vision of creating a unique synergy between premium medical services and world-class hospitality. At MedPark, every traveler can feel reassured, whether needing urgent medical care, a quick check-up, or advanced aesthetic treatments, our international-standard facilities and multilingual medical teams are always ready.

This partnership goes beyond improving patient satisfaction. It represents a shared commitment to strengthening Egypt’s role in global medical tourism by aligning healthcare, hospitality, and tourism development. Together with trusted partners, we are setting new benchmarks for guest wellbeing where safety, comfort, and luxury go hand in hand.

We extend our gratitude to the leadership of EALB, EDIT, and Long Beach Resort for their trust and recognition. Their visit inspires us to continue developing an integrated model of healthcare and hospitality, ensuring that Egypt’s Red Sea region becomes a leading destination for international travelers seeking both peace of mind and premium medical services.
