New Hub, New Destination: HCIG Launches Premium Wellness Clinic in Marassi, North Coast

Marassi, North Coast, Egypt. Healthcare International Group (HCIG) is proud to unveil its latest milestone in integrated health and tourism, a dedicated wellness destination in Marassi, on Egypt’s iconic North Coast. Set against the backdrop of pristine beaches and vibrant summer life, this new hub brings together medical excellence and holistic wellness, creating a first-of-its-kind experience tailored for tourists and seasonal residents.

This project is more than a clinic, it’s a concept. At its heart lies the belief that health and wellbeing should be seamlessly woven into the travel experience. Whether visiting for a weekend escape or an extended stay, guests will have access to advanced wellness services designed to support rest, recovery, and rejuvenation. From hydrating IV therapies and revitalizing facial treatments to cutting-edge wellness technologies, the Marassi clinic will offer personalized care that feels both effortless and luxurious.

The launch in Marassi marks just the beginning. HCIG is committed to establishing a network of wellness hubs across the North Coast, bringing high-quality wellness services closer to every major resort and compound in the region. Our goal is to make world-class wellness easily accessible for all travelers and residents seeking relaxation with the confidence of clinical expertise.

This new destination reflects a broader vision led by Dr. Amr Abbass and Dr. Mostafa Elshakankiry, two pioneers shaping the future of Egypt’s healthcare landscape. Their leadership continues to drive HCIG’s mission to reimagine healthcare delivery, not only for residents but for global travelers seeking high standards of care while abroad.

The North Coast, long known for its beauty, is now taking its place as a wellness destination. With HCIG’s presence starting in Marassi, the region is poised to welcome a new wave of visitors, those who see wellness not as a luxury, but as an essential part of how they travel, rest, and live.

At HCIG, we believe wellness is the future of tourism. And we’re proud to be building that future, one hub at a time, right here in Egypt.
