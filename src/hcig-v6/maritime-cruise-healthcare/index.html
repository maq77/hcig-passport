Maritime & Cruise Healthcare Solutions

Healthcare International Group (HCIG) extends its integrated healthcare ecosystem beyond hospitals, clinics, and resorts into the maritime industry, providing cruise lines, ship operators, and maritime partners with comprehensive medical solutions both onboard and ashore.

Integrated Onboard Medical Operations | Medical Staffing | Shore-Side Healthcare | 24/7 Medical Assistance

From establishing fully equipped medical facilities onboard cruise vessels and recruiting qualified doctors and nurses to managing clinical operations, coordinating emergency treatment in port, and arranging international medical evacuation, HCIG brings every stage of maritime healthcare into one connected operational structure.

We do not simply connect vessels with medical providers. We help maritime operators build, manage, and sustain the healthcare systems their passengers and crew depend on.

One Healthcare Partner. From Vessel to Shore.

ONE INTEGRATED MARITIME HEALTHCARE ECOSYSTEM

Healthcare Should Not Stop Where the Shore Ends.

A cruise vessel is a moving community, carrying passengers and crew across different countries, medical jurisdictions, and healthcare systems.

When a medical situation arises, onboard medical teams, shore-side hospitals, emergency transport providers, insurers, and port agents must operate together, often under significant time constraints.

HCIG brings these elements into one coordinated healthcare ecosystem, combining medical infrastructure, qualified professionals, clinical management, international assistance, and access to shore-side healthcare networks.

Our approach is designed to support clinical continuity, operational efficiency, and a consistent standard of medical care throughout the maritime journey.

Turnkey Onboard Medical Clinics

From clinic establishment to daily medical operations.

HCIG supports cruise operators in developing and operating onboard medical facilities tailored to vessel requirements, passenger capacity, crew composition, and operational needs.

Our capabilities include:

  • Onboard clinic planning and setup
  • Medical infrastructure and equipment coordination
  • Clinical workflows and operating procedures
  • Medical supplies, medication, and inventory coordination
  • Infection prevention and emergency preparedness
  • Medical documentation and reporting systems
  • Day-to-day clinic operations and clinical oversight
  • Integration with telemedicine and shore-side healthcare services

Whether establishing a new medical facility or restructuring an existing onboard operation, HCIG provides a coordinated model covering the clinical and operational functions of the service.

Medical Recruitment & Staffing

From one medical professional to a complete onboard medical team.

HCIG supports maritime operators with the recruitment, selection, credential verification, and deployment of qualified healthcare professionals for cruise vessels and other maritime environments.

Our medical staffing solutions cover:

  • Recruitment of doctors and nurses
  • International medical recruitment
  • Professional qualification and credential verification
  • Medical team composition and staffing plans
  • Staff onboarding and deployment coordination
  • Rotational workforce planning
  • Replacement and continuity arrangements
  • Clinical supervision and operational support

Staffing solutions can be provided independently or as part of a fully managed onboard medical service.

Onboard Medical Operations & Fleet Management

One consistent healthcare model across vessels and fleets.

For cruise operators managing multiple vessels, maintaining consistency in healthcare delivery requires more than placing medical professionals onboard.

HCIG supports centralized medical operations management, enabling cruise operators to implement coordinated clinical procedures, staffing models, documentation systems, medical escalation pathways, and reporting structures.

Our operating model includes:

  • Centralized medical operations coordination
  • Standardized clinical procedures
  • Multi-vessel medical staffing management
  • Medical equipment and supply coordination
  • Telemedicine and specialist support
  • Medical reporting and quality monitoring
  • Shore-side healthcare network integration
  • Medical escalation and emergency coordination

The result is a scalable medical management structure that can be adapted to individual vessels, fleets, and international operating environments.

Shore-Side Medical Assistance & Port Healthcare

When medical care must continue ashore, the connection is already there.

Through HCIG's international healthcare infrastructure and medical assistance capabilities, cruise passengers and crew can be supported when medical assessment, diagnostic investigations, specialist treatment, or hospitalization is required in port.

Our shore-side services include:

  • 24/7 medical assistance and case coordination
  • Access to doctors, specialists, clinics, and hospitals
  • Emergency department and hospital admission coordination
  • Laboratory testing and diagnostic imaging
  • Ambulance and medical transportation
  • Medical escorts and translation
  • Communication with port agents, vessel representatives, and insurers
  • Medical documentation and reporting
  • Return-to-vessel coordination
  • Hospitalized passenger and crew follow-up

Medical routing takes into account both the patient's clinical requirements and the vessel's operational schedule, helping partners make informed decisions without compromising appropriate care.

Insurance, Medical Evacuation & Repatriation

Continuity of care, even when the journey changes.

A medical case does not necessarily end when a vessel departs.

When a passenger or crew member requires extended treatment, hospitalization, evacuation, or repatriation, HCIG coordinates the healthcare and operational arrangements required to continue the patient's journey safely.

Services include:

  • Insurance verification and assistance company coordination
  • Guarantee of Payment (GOP) coordination
  • Medical case documentation
  • Cost-containment support
  • Hospital discharge and treatment follow-up
  • Fit-to-Travel and Fit-for-Duty coordination
  • Ground and air medical transport arrangements
  • Medical escort services
  • International medical evacuation
  • Medical repatriation

Our responsibility is to maintain coordination between the patient, vessel, medical providers, insurers, and relevant partners until the case reaches its appropriate resolution.

Yachting, Superyachts & Marina Healthcare

Personalized healthcare for the private maritime environment.

HCIG also supports yacht owners, guests, captains, crew, and marina operators with discreet, personalized medical solutions.

These services can include:

  • Doctor-to-yacht and doctor-to-marina coordination
  • Private medical consultations
  • Access to specialists and diagnostic facilities
  • Telemedicine and remote specialist support
  • Emergency medical assistance
  • Private hospital coordination
  • Medical concierge and preventive healthcare
  • Medical transportation, evacuation, and repatriation

Our approach combines clinical coordination with the flexibility, discretion, and responsiveness expected in the luxury maritime environment.
