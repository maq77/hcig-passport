Foundations first, then seventy-four screens in the order a real user meets them: onboarding, signing in, finding care, being treated, the record, paying, and every empty, offline and error state in between. A

Onboarding & identity

Sixteen screens. Onboarding leads with the group — 100+ facilities, 40 years, five countries and three international accreditations — before the app asks for anything. The whole job here is to create one HCIG ID and to recognise a returning guest — everything else can wait until the person actually needs care. Apple and Google sign-in are offered before any form, because a guest in pain will not type a password.

B

Home & finding care

Nine screens. The urgent path is never more than one tap from the home screen, and every facility is ranked by capability, language and cashless status — not by distance alone. Reviews and patient videos are real, pulled from HCIG's own published testimonials.

C

Requesting care

Seven screens, shown in German because that is the largest source market. Structured triage sets the urgency tier and pre-fills the doctor's note before anyone leaves the clinic.

D

Consultation & discharge

Five screens. Consent is captured at the bedside in the guest's own language, and the visit ends with a document their doctor at home can actually read.

E

The health record

Eight screens. One timeline across every HCIG clinic, health hub and hospital, with a clinical alert banner that never scrolls away and an export their own doctor can read.

F

Family & dependants

Three screens. Families are the majority of resort guests, so one install should capture four or five patient identities rather than one.

G

Care Pass & booking

Seven screens. From here on the frames are shown in English — the same screens with the language setting changed, so the localisation holds up in both directions. Commercial surfaces never appear on a clinical screen.

H

Teleconsult

Three screens. Works before the trip, during it, and — most valuably — on day five after the guest has flown home.

I

Coverage & payment

Three screens. Cashless status is the single thing a worried guest wants confirmed before they agree to treatment.

J

Account & settings

Five screens. Consent withdrawal and data export live here as first-class actions, not buried in a legal page — that is a requirement, not a courtesy.

K

Staff tools

Four tablet screens. The guest app wins the relationship; these win the money — documentation quality, claim completeness and the routing decisions that stop a language gap becoming a clinical risk.

L

System states

Four screens that decide whether people trust the product. Resort connectivity is unreliable and always will be, so offline is a designed state — not an error.
