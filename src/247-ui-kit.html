The brief, as rules

Six rules this kit obeys

Every one comes from the brief. They are here because a design system that forgets the brief is decoration.

01

The phone number sits above the fold

No corporate scroll before it. Call, WhatsApp and Find the clinic are the first things under the headline.

02

Mobile is the design, not the adaptation

Drawn at 390 pixels wide, then allowed to grow. A thumb reaches every action without stretching.

03

No two pages are the same page

The template holds the layout. The content table holds the words, and every hotel writes its own.

04

Do not overdesign

Three colours, one typeface, outline icons, real photographs. No gradients, no glass, no illustration.

05

Emergency red, WhatsApp green

The two colours a distressed guest already knows. Nothing else competes with them on the page.

06

Nothing unverified passes silently

Anything still waiting on an answer is marked on the page and listed at the bottom of it.

Rule

Conversion and local search, not a brochure. If an element does not help a guest call, find or trust the clinic, it does not ship.

Colour

Three colours carry the whole page

Classic Red is the brand and the primary action. WhatsApp green is the only other saturated colour allowed. Everything else is a warm neutral, so the two that matter always win.

Classic Red#C00000Brand, primary button, section kickers, icons. From the 24/7 Clinic guideline.
WhatsApp green#1FA855WhatsApp only. Never a decorative green.
Ink#131110Body text and the Directions button. Warm black, not pure black.
Sand#F0ECE7Alternate section ground. Reads as resort, not as hospital.
Checked

Ink on white measures 17.2:1. White on Classic Red measures 6.5:1. White on WhatsApp green measures 3.6:1, which is why that button never carries text smaller than 17 pixels at 600 weight. In dark mode the red text lifts to #FF5A5A, because #C00000 on a dark ground fails as text. Filled buttons do not lift: they keep #C00000 and #1FA855 in both themes, because lifting them dropped white on red to 2.8:1.

Type

Poppins does the work

The guideline sets Poppins as the main face and reserves Calisto MT for the tagline and headlines. On the web Calisto MT is not a licensed web font, so it is used only for the tagline, with Georgia behind it. Nothing structural depends on it loading.

Rule

Prose stops at about 62 characters. Tables, diagrams and photo grids break out to full width inside their own scrolling container, so the page never scrolls sideways.

Icons

One weight, one colour, no exceptions

Outline only, 1.9 stroke, drawn on a 24 grid and coloured by whatever sits behind them. That is why the same file works on a red button, a pink tile and a dark background without a second copy.

Page anatomy

What a phone shows, in order

The fold is drawn where a 390 by 844 phone cuts the page. Everything above it exists to get a guest to press one of three buttons.

Photography

Their own clinics, nothing else

Fifteen stills, pulled from 24/7 Clinic's own films. No stock, no illustration, no borrowed hospital. The alt text names the place, because a page that lies in its alt text lies to Google too.

Open

Every one of these was shot at the Sahl Hasheesh clinic. Steigenberger Ras Soma and Amwaj Beach Club have no photographs of their own yet, so their pages borrow these and say so in the caption. That is a launch blocker, not a nice to have.

Notes live elsewhere

The pages carry no notes at all

A landing page under review should read exactly as it will read to a guest. No markers, no placeholders, no editor's voice. Anything still open is written down in one place instead.

On the page

Only what a guest needs

Every claim on the three pages is either taken from the clinic's own published material or checked against an independent source. Where a fact was missing, the copy was written so it does not need one, rather than left as a gap.

The Premier Le Rêve walking route, for example, is quoted from the clinic's own directions film, word for word.

In its own document

Ten open items, with evidence

Which of two published phone numbers to use, the wrong map pin in their database, photographs for two clinics, the GA4 property, and the four site bugs the launch depends on.

Rule

A demo is shown as if it were live. Review notes go in the document, never on the page.

One template

Three pages today, twenty eight without new code

The three demos are generated from a single template plus a content table. Adding a clinic is a data entry. The schema, the canonical, the tracking and the layout are written once, so quality cannot drift across the network.

Tracking

Every tap is a comparable number

Each contact element carries a data attribute. One listener sends the event with the hotel and area attached, so a single GA4 report ranks all twenty eight clinics against each other.
