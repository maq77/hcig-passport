Questions

What guests ask us

Is there a doctor at Amwaj Beach Club?
Yes. 24/7 Clinic serves guests of the resort. You can call, send a WhatsApp message, or walk in.
Are you open at night?
Open 24 hours. Call the number on this page at any hour.
Do you speak English?
Yes. Our doctors see international guests every day. German, Russian and Polish are spoken across the network.
Do you take my travel insurance?
We handle the paperwork with your insurer and write the medical report your claim needs. Bring your policy details or your insurance card when you come.
What does a visit cost?
Ask when you call. There is no charge for the blood pressure and blood sugar check.
Can I get a medical report for my insurance?
Yes. A written report is prepared at the clinic, in English, before you fly home.
What if I need a hospital?
We arrange the ambulance and the referral. 24/7 Clinic is part of Healthcare International Group, which runs its own hospitals on the Red Sea coast.
