HCIG at the 17th Annual Global Insurance Conference by Uniglobal

Barcelona, Spain – Healthcare International Group (HCIG) had the privilege of participating in the 17th Annual Global Insurance Conference by Uniglobal, held from 23–25 September 2025. Representing both HCIG and Egypt, our CEO Dr. Amr Abbass joined global leaders in insurance, healthcare, and assistance to explore new pathways for international collaboration.

The conference provided a powerful platform to highlight Egypt’s growing role in global healthcare and medical tourism. Dr. Abbass’s contribution reflected HCIG’s vision of building a trusted international healthcare network — one that seamlessly connects insurers, providers, and patients across borders.

Our participation underscored the importance of creating strong partnerships between healthcare and insurance stakeholders. By aligning medical expertise with insurance solutions and patient needs, HCIG is setting new benchmarks for trust, accessibility, and global healthcare integration.

This milestone is part of our broader commitment to position Egypt as a reliable partner in international healthcare and medical tourism. Through our expanding network of hospitals, on-site 24/7 clinics, and medical tourism services, we continue to deliver care that meets international standards while ensuring peace of mind for travelers and patients worldwide.

We extend our gratitude to Uniglobal for hosting such an impactful event and for recognizing HCIG’s role in advancing healthcare cooperation on a global scale. Our presence in Barcelona inspires us to keep developing innovative, patient-centered solutions that strengthen Egypt’s standing in the global healthcare landscape.
