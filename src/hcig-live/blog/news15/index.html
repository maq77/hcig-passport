24/7 Clinics Achieve UCA Accreditation in Chicago: A Milestone for Global Urgent Care

Chicago, USA - 24/7 Clinics, part of Healthcare International Group (HCIG), has reached a defining milestone in its international development, becoming the first urgent care organization outside the United States to be accredited by the Urgent Care Association (UCA) through the Commission on Ambulatory and Urgent Care Quality (CAUCQ).

The accreditation was formally recognized in Chicago, placing 24/7 Clinics among a highly selective group of providers operating under globally recognized urgent care standards - standards originally developed within one of the most advanced and structured healthcare systems in the world.

This achievement reflects more than compliance. It confirms that a model built and operated internationally can meet the same level of clinical discipline, operational consistency, and system accountability expected within the United States.

At the core of this recognition is the way 24/7 Clinics has been designed from the beginning. Rather than functioning as standalone medical points, the network operates as part of an integrated system developed by HCIG - one that is built to perform in real-world conditions where urgency, complexity, and coordination define outcomes.

Within this model, urgent care is not limited to immediate treatment. It is structured as a complete pathway that includes triage, clinical decision-making, escalation when required, and direct coordination with hospitals, insurance companies, and assistance providers. Each stage follows clearly defined protocols aligned with international standards.

This is precisely what UCA accreditation evaluates. It does not focus solely on facilities or individual clinical expertise, but on whether healthcare delivery is consistent, whether patient safety is embedded in operations, and whether systems can sustain performance under pressure.

For 24/7 Clinics, this validation is particularly significant within the context of travel medicine. Unlike traditional healthcare environments, international patient care operates across unfamiliar systems, limited timeframes, and multiple stakeholders. In such conditions, the absence of structure often leads to delays, miscommunication, and fragmented care.

By aligning with UCA standards, 24/7 Clinics addresses this challenge directly. It creates a controlled and predictable environment where patient journeys are managed with clarity, speed, and accountability - from the first point of contact through to resolution.

This approach strengthens the role of 24/7 Clinics within the global travel medical ecosystem. For insurance companies and assistance providers, it ensures that clinical and operational processes follow a consistent framework. For hospitality partners, it provides reliable on-site urgent care that integrates seamlessly into the guest experience. And for patients, it delivers something that is often missing in cross-border healthcare - confidence.

The recognition in Chicago marks a moment where this model is not only implemented, but independently validated.

It also contributes to a broader shift in how international healthcare is perceived. As markets like Egypt continue to evolve within medical tourism, the ability to demonstrate globally recognized standards becomes essential - not only in clinical capability, but in system performance.

For 24/7 Clinics, this milestone reflects the work of teams across the entire network, consistently operating under demanding conditions and maintaining alignment with international protocols.

Because in urgent care, outcomes are not defined by intention, but by execution.

The accreditation achieved in Chicago confirms that 24/7 Clinics delivers both.
