HCIG at ITIC Global Venice 2025: Egypt’s Healthcare Reimagined

Venice, Italy – Healthcare International Group (HCIG) proudly participated in ITIC Global 2025, one of the world’s most prominent gatherings for travel and health insurance professionals, held this November in the timeless city of Venice, Italy.

Representing the Group were Dr. Amr Abbass, Chief Executive Officer, and Irina Rise, Marketing Director — both attending to strengthen Egypt’s presence on the global healthcare and assistance map.

Throughout the conference, HCIG presented its unique national healthcare model, built to support tourists and travelers across Egypt’s top destinations. With a network of more than 150 medical facilities, including hospitals, urgent care centers, and resort-based clinics strategically located in the Red Sea, Sinai, and North Coast regions, HCIG ensures immediate access to quality healthcare — right where travelers need it most.

HCIG’s model of “Insta-Med” — instant medical response inside hotels and resorts — delivers rapid, on-the-spot medical care that not only saves patients from complications but also reduces financial risk for insurance companies. Every minute of delay in emergency response increases the risk of deterioration, medical complications, and significant cost escalation for insurers.

By ensuring a fast and professional medical reaction, HCIG helps prevent these risks, protecting both the patient’s health and the insurer’s resources.

“In healthcare, time is life — and collaboration is the bridge that saves it.”
– Dr. Amr Abbass

Immediate urgent care remains the most cost-effective solution in cases of emergencies and critical conditions — a principle that HCIG applies across every region where it operates.

HCIG continues to lead with innovation, commitment, and global vision — bringing healthcare closer to people, saving lives, and setting a new international standard for medical accessibility in tourist destinations.
