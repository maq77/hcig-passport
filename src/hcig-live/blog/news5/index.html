HCIG and GHA Partner to Elevate Egypt as a Premier Medical and Wellness Travel Destination

Cairo, Egypt – In a milestone achievement for Egypt’s healthcare and tourism sectors, Healthcare International Group (HCIG) has entered into a strategic partnership with Global Healthcare Accreditation (GHA), one of the world’s leading organizations in medical travel standards. This collaboration marks a significant step in positioning Egypt as a top destination for medical and wellness tourism across the MENA region.

By integrating GHA’s internationally recognized accreditation standards into its facilities and programs, HCIG aims to transform the patient experience and build international trust in the quality, safety, and transparency of healthcare delivery in Egypt.

The partnership brings together GHA’s global expertise in healthcare quality and patient safety with HCIG’s extensive footprint of hospitals, clinics, urgent care centers, pharmacies, and wellness facilities across Egypt. Together, the two organizations will implement best practices in clinical operations, service excellence, and patient-centered care to meet the growing expectations of both local and international medical travelers.

Dr. Amr Abbass, Chairman and CEO of HCIG, commented on the significance of the partnership: “This partnership is a strategic step toward transforming Egypt into a world-class medical and wellness destination. At HCIG, we are proud to align with GHA in setting new global standards; ensuring that every patient, whether local or international, receives care defined by safety, excellence, and trust.”

The partnership is also expected to play a vital role in strengthening institutional capabilities across Egypt’s healthcare ecosystem. GHA will support HCIG and affiliated providers through training programs, performance assessments, and a robust certification process focused not only on clinical care, but on the holistic patient journey—from the first inquiry to post-treatment follow-up.

Renée-Marie Stephano, CEO of Global Healthcare Accreditation, expressed her enthusiasm for the collaboration, stating: “We are excited to partner with HCIG, whose leadership and commitment to excellence align seamlessly with GHA’s mission. This collaboration allows us to jointly boost the standard of care for medical travelers and position Egypt as a global model for safe, high-quality, and patient-centric medical tourism.”

With medical tourism to Egypt growing rapidly, including a 200% increase in 2024 alone, this partnership comes at a pivotal moment. HCIG and GHA are uniquely positioned to guide the sector’s development through a unified vision of quality, accountability, and international relevance.

This initiative reflects a long-term strategy to strengthen Egypt’s healthcare infrastructure while advancing its reputation on the global stage. As the partnership unfolds, it is set to deliver not only economic benefits to the region, but also a new level of healthcare access and excellence for patients from around the world.
