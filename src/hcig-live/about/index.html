About Us

Innovating Healthcare, Changing Lives.

Talk to our team
Secure & confidential communication

With over 40 years of experience, Healthcare International Group (HCIG) is built to manage the full patient journey across international healthcare markets.

Rather than operating as a traditional healthcare provider, HCIG brings structure to a system that is often fragmented connecting urgent care, hospital treatment, and international medical coordination within one clear model.

Our ecosystem spans on-site medical services in hotels and resorts, advanced hospital care, and direct coordination with insurance and assistance partners.

This approach allows us to ensure:

  • faster access to care
  • clear responsibility across the case
  • seamless patient management from first contact to resolution

Operating across multiple markets, HCIG supports international patients, insurers, and global partners with structured, reliable, and scalable healthcare solutions.

We don’t just provide healthcare we manage it.

Our mission

To build and scale a healthcare ecosystem that redefines how patient care is accessed, managed, and delivered across international markets.

To take full responsibility for the patient journey ensuring speed, clarity, and control from the first point of contact to resolution.

To lead the transformation of healthcare by working with insurers, providers, and global partners to eliminate fragmentation and establish a more efficient, transparent, and globally connected model of care.



Our vision

To define the future of global healthcare by setting a new standard for how medical services are delivered across borders.

We envision a world where healthcare is no longer fragmented or reactive, but structured, predictable, and seamlessly embedded into global travel, hospitality, and everyday life.

Our Healthcare

Delivered across multiple levels of care within one structured system.

  • Hospital & Specialty Services

    Advanced inpatient and outpatient care delivered through our hospital network - supported by full diagnostic capabilities, laboratory services, imaging, and specialized medical treatment across multiple disciplines.

  • Medical Tourism Facilitation

    End-to-end patient journey management for international patients - from initial coordination to treatment, travel logistics, medication support, and post-care follow-up.

  • Emergency & Urgent Care

    24/7 immediate medical access through on-site clinics, urgent care centers, and integrated pharmacy support - ensuring fast treatment and medication availability at the point of care.

  • Wellness, Longevity & Aesthetic Care

    Preventive, recovery, and lifestyle-focused medical services designed to support long-term health, wellbeing, and performance.

  • Corporate Healthcare Solutions

    Tailored healthcare programs for organizations, insurers, and international partners - ensuring access, continuity, and efficient care management across global operations.

  • Elite Medical Concierge Service

    High-touch, personalized medical support with full coordination across all HCIG services, ensuring a seamless and stress-free patient experience.

  • Accreditation & Training Services

    Supporting healthcare providers through training, operational consulting, and accreditation pathways to meet international standards and integrate into global medical networks.
