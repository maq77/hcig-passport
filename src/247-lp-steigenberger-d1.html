Questions

What guests ask us

Is there a doctor at Steigenberger Ras Soma?
Yes, and you do not need an appointment. Call, send a WhatsApp message, or walk in.
Are you open at night?
Yes. The clinic is open 24 hours, every day of the year.
Do you speak English?
Yes. Guests have also been treated and answered in German, Italian and French.
Do you take my travel insurance?
We deal with your insurer and write the medical report your claim needs. Bring your policy details or your insurance card.
Is there a hospital near Soma Bay?
If you need a hospital we arrange the ambulance and the referral. 24/7 Clinic is part of Healthcare International Group, which runs its own hospitals on the Red Sea coast.
