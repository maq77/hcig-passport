Travel Medical Insurance in Egypt

Accessing Healthcare Without Financial Stress

18 Jun 2026

Travelling to Egypt offers an opportunity to enjoy some of the world’s most beautiful coastal destinations, from the vibrant resorts of Hurghada and Sharm El Sheikh to the peaceful beaches of Marsa Alam and the Mediterranean North Coast. However, even a carefully planned holiday can be interrupted by an unexpected illness or injury, making access to reliable medical care an essential part of the travel experience.

Travel medical insurance plays an important role in protecting international travellers from unexpected healthcare expenses abroad. Depending on the individual policy, it can provide financial coverage for medical consultations, diagnostic examinations, prescribed medication, emergency treatment and hospitalisation.

Making travel insurance work for international patients

One of the most common challenges tourists face when seeking medical treatment abroad is navigating the insurance process. Unfamiliar administrative procedures, language barriers and uncertainty about medical expenses can create additional stress at a time when patients should be focusing on their recovery.

24/7 Clinics works with international travel insurance and medical assistance companies to simplify access to healthcare for tourists visiting Egypt. Our team assists patients with insurance verification, medical documentation and communication with their insurance providers, reducing the administrative burden associated with receiving treatment abroad.

Through direct billing arrangements, eligible patients may receive medical treatment without having to pay covered medical expenses upfront, subject to the terms of their insurance policy and confirmation of coverage.

This approach is particularly valuable for international travellers who would otherwise need to pay for treatment themselves and submit reimbursement claims after returning home.

Combining medical care with insurance assistance

The integration of medical services and insurance coordination allows patients to access a more organised healthcare experience. From the initial medical consultation to diagnostic procedures, follow-up care and hospital referrals when necessary, our team helps coordinate both the medical and administrative aspects of treatment.

With clinics located inside selected hotels and resorts across Egypt, 24/7 Clinics brings professional medical care closer to international travellers. Patients can access healthcare within familiar surroundings, while our team assists with the necessary insurance procedures.

Our network supports tourists across Hurghada, Sahl Hasheesh, Safaga, El Quseir, Marsa Alam, Sharm El Sheikh and Egypt’s North Coast.

Whether travelling independently, enjoying a family holiday or visiting Egypt as part of an organised tour, having access to a medical provider experienced in working with international travel insurance can make an unexpected medical situation significantly easier to manage.

At 24/7 Clinics, our approach extends beyond medical treatment itself. By combining accessible healthcare, insurance coordination and international patient support, we help travellers receive the care they need with greater confidence and fewer unnecessary complications.
