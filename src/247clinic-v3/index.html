On-site care

Medical Care Without Leaving Your Resort

Ill or injured on holiday? A hospital shouldn’t always be your first stop.

  1. 01

    Inside Your Resort

    No searching for an unfamiliar medical facility across the city. Care is already where you are staying.

  2. 02

    Treated On-Site

    Many urgent and primary care conditions are assessed and treated right here, with consultation, diagnostics and treatment close to your room.

  3. 03

    Hospital Only When Needed

    If you need more, our team arranges the right hospital, ambulance or next medical step.

How it works

Getting Medical Help Is Simple

  1. Step 01

    Message Us on WhatsApp

    Tell us your hotel and what happened.

  2. Step 02

    We Find the Nearest Medical Option

    If your hotel has a 24/7 Clinic, we direct you there. If not, our coordination team finds the most appropriate medical option nearby.

  3. Step 03

    We Check Your Insurance

    Send us your insurance details and, where applicable, we coordinate directly with your insurer.

  4. Step 04

    Receive Medical Care

    Treatment on-site whenever clinically appropriate.
