How it works

Getting Medical Help Is Simple

  1. Step 01

    Message Us on WhatsApp

    Tell us your hotel and what happened.

  2. Step 02

    We Arrange Your Medical Visit

    If your hotel has a 24/7 Clinic, we direct you there. If not, we promptly send our agent to pick you up and take you to the nearest 24/7 Clinic.

  3. Step 03

    Send us your insurance details and, where applicable, we coordinate directly with your insurer.

    Receive Medical Care
