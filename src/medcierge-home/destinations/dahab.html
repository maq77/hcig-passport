Dahab, answered

Do you come to my hotel in Dahab?

Yes. Our doctors visit guests at any hotel in Dahab, day and night. Call or message us with your hotel and room number.

Do I have to pay the doctor?

No. We bill your travel insurer directly, so you pay nothing. Have your policy number ready when you call.

Which languages do you speak?

Our coordinators speak your language, and we have German-speaking and Polish-speaking doctors in our main towns.

What if I need a hospital?

Most problems are treated at your hotel. If you need a hospital, we arrange the transfer to a partner hospital and coordinate your care and your insurer.
