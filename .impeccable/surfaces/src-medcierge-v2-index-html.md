# Medcierge design 2 (lapis and gold)

Scope: the medcierge.com rebuild, design 2, all pages, home page first. Visitor mode: persuade.
Audience and job: a hotel guest in Egypt who feels unwell wants a doctor in the room, in their language, now. Action: call or WhatsApp; secondary, request a callback. Proof: 12 destinations, partner hotels and insurers, Official Partner of GHA and DMWV. Constraints: PRODUCT.md; English, German, Polish; light and dark mode; red emergency and green WhatsApp untouched.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Medcierge as an Egyptian temple ceiling, lapis night painted with gold stars, where every star is a town a doctor reaches tonight. It refuses the white-and-teal clinic template and the cream-and-serif luxury default.

OWN-WORLD: cool alabaster ground, lapis blue owning type and whole panels, gold leaf only as hairlines, seals and star points, faience turquoise for live states. Marcellus inscription capitals over Manrope text. Incised hairline frames, cartouche ovals around place names, placard plates for labels. Dark mode is the ceiling itself.

STORY: a calm five-star moment, then the promise (a doctor to your room, 24/7, in your language), then proof, then the star chart where they find their town, then one tap to a human.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left, a two-line inscription-capital headline, one short line, gold primary action and outline secondary; the red emergency line in the header. Right, a tall temple-door arch framing the suite photograph in three depth planes: gold star field behind, arch and photo, a floating on-call placard in front.

FORM: lapis and gold ceiling, position 5 of 7 on the grounded list, seed a6dc5bcc. Signature: the service map as a star chart whose route draws like a constellation, the chosen town sealed in a cartouche. Raises: from the night market sign river, the emergency line is always the nearest sign on every screen; from the brine calendar, each town states its hours in words; from the step row, how-it-works is one unbroken timeline with a light marking now.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
