# Verbal Identity

Brand Essence - Brand Promise - Tone of Voice - Key Brand Messages - Elevator Pitch - Slogan Ideas

## Brand Essence (page 20)

**Trusted to set global standards in Egypt’s healthcare.**

HCIG is the trusted medical assistance and medical tourism partner in Egypt — working with Global Healthcare
Accreditation (GHA) to bring international standards to every patient journey.

## Brand Promise (page 21)

**We don’t just provide medical care, we earn trust by setting the highest standards.**

As the official GHA partner in Egypt, HCIG ensures that hospitals, patients, and global partners experience care that
is safe, reliable, and world-class.

## Tone of Voice (page 22)

- **Trusted & Reassuring:** Our words convey confidence without arrogance.
- **Standard-Setter:** We guide the industry with credibility and vision.
- **Human & Compassionate:** We always put the patient experience at the center.
- **Visionary:** We talk about the future of Egypt’s role in global healthcare.

## Key Brand Messages (page 23)

1. Trusted Partnership: HCIG is the trusted partner of GHA in Egypt, ensuring medical tourism is built on international
   standards.
2. Trust Through Action: Our network of hospitals and clinics consistently proves quality through patient experience.
3. Trusted by Patients, Partners & Governments: From tourists to embassies, insurers, and ministries — stakeholders rely
   on HCIG.
4. Global Standards, Local Trust: We bring world-class healthcare to Egypt while building confidence in every patient
   touchpoint.
5. Trust is Our Reputation: In healthcare, reputation is built through trust — and HCIG leads by example.

## Elevator Pitch (page 24)

HCIG is Egypt’s most trusted name in medical assistance and medical tourism. As the official partner of Global
Healthcare Accreditation (GHA), we bring international standards into every hospital, clinic, and patient journey.
With a nationwide network and a commitment to excellence, HCIG ensures that every patient, tourist, and partner
experiences healthcare they can trust — anytime, anywhere.

## Slogan Ideas, with TRUST (page 25)

- HCIG — Trusted to Set Standards.
- Global Standards. Local Trust.
- HCIG — Where Trust Meets Care.
- Trusted Care, Anytime. Anywhere.
- Building Trust. Raising Standards.
