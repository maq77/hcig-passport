I would also make these implementation decisions:

- Link to this new page from the homepage, Services, and For Partners pages.
- Add a dedicated contact form with fields for Company, Fleet/Vessel Type, Required Services, Operating Region, and Contact Details.
- Keep the form and website branding under HCIG, but describe TMASI Global as the group's medical assistance and case coordination capability.
- Use *Healthcare International Group* throughout the public copy; do not simply replace the TMASI logo on the brochure and publish it as HCIG.

One important distinction: the brochure describes an extensive range of **capabilities and partnership models**, but it does not establish how many cruise clinics HCIG has already opened or how many fleets it currently manages. The website copy therefore deliberately presents these as services HCIG *can provide*, rather than making unverified claims about existing onboard contracts.
