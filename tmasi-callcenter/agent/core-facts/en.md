# TMASI Core Facts

These are the only facts the assistant may state about TMASI. Anything not here or in the searched knowledge is not known, and a coordinator follows up.

## Who TMASI is

- Name: TMASI Global, Travel Medical Assistance Services International.
- What TMASI does: medical, travel, insurance and tourism assistance for travellers worldwide, with case coordination.
- The business in one line: medical plus tourism plus vacation, which is medical tourism. Guests and patients have their treatment and their holiday at the same time, in premium hotels, with world-class healthcare.
- Slogan: "Your Care. One Call Away." Also: "Care Without Borders".
- Mission: to redefine the standard of safety and care for travellers worldwide, connecting global partners with reliable local medical networks and on-the-ground support.
- Vision: a future where healthcare, insurance and travel work hand in hand.
- Published figures: more than 30,000 cases handled, more than 570 medical repatriations, a 24/7 operational desk, 5 global operational hubs.

## Services (six groups)

1. **Medical Tourism Services**: healthcare packages combining medical care in Egypt, Germany, Spain and the UAE with premium hospitality.
    - Comprehensive healthcare packages: wellness, longevity, recovery programs, full body check-ups, second opinions, specialized surgeries.
    - Top medical destinations: hospitals, clinics, rehabilitation centers and physicians in Germany, Spain, Egypt and the UAE.
    - Personalized travel arrangements: medical visas, private transport, luxury accommodation, post-treatment care.
    - Dedicated coordinators: one point of contact for every clinical and travel detail of the journey.
    - Luxury and comfort options: optional spa treatments, sightseeing and wellness retreats next to the treatment.
2. **Medical Assistance Services**: emergency medical assistance around the clock; air and ground medical evacuations; hospital and treatment coordination; case management and follow-up; evaluation and review; funeral services and repatriation of mortal remains.
3. **Elite Medical Concierge Services**: doctor on call at hotels, residences or workplaces; private medical visits; portable diagnostics during visits; VIP patient care; doctor visits coordinated with international insurers for cashless treatment when coverage allows; follow-up medical support.
4. **Travel Assistance Services**: translation assistance; advance cash in emergencies; roadside assistance and car replacement; taxi bookings; hotel and flight reservations and rebooking; legal consultations and support.
5. **Insurance Assistance Services**: insurance issue resolution; claim management; policy verification; direct billing with insurers for cashless settlement; liaison with insurance companies.
6. **Additional Services**: travel risk assessments; documentation and paperwork (medical reports, consular certifications, official translations); customized corporate support; repatriation of mortal remains.

## How a medical holiday works with TMASI (from the services above)

1. The guest tells a TMASI coordinator what they need and when.
2. A dedicated coordinator plans the treatment and the travel together: hospital or clinic, visa if needed, transport, hotel.
3. TMASI arranges the journey and stays the single point of contact during treatment and holiday.
4. Follow-up care after the treatment.
