SEO, GEO, AEO and PageSpeed

Search, AI and speed

Built in from the first page. Never added at the end.

Technical search

  • On every page One H1, unique title and description, canonical, hreflang for seven languages plus x-default, share image per language, breadcrumbs, semantic navigation.
  • Structured data as one graph HCIG as the parent organisation, the eight brands as sub-organisations with their own sites, the board as people, services, news articles, events, FAQs where approved.
  • Addresses A sitemap per language, a redirect map for every old address, a designed 404.
  • Engines Search Console, plus Bing Webmaster Tools and IndexNow. Bing feeds ChatGPT search and Copilot.

Being named by AI tools

  • Baseline first 30 fixed prompts in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot, plus Google AI Overviews checks. Re-run every month.
  • Answer blocks Key pages open with two short sentences that answer the buyer's question. New copy, approved first.
  • A fact sheet AI tools can quote Name, brands, board, addresses, partnerships, numbers. Plus llms.txt.
  • One truth everywhere Same name spelling, same board titles on the site, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Wikidata, directories and the group sites.

Needed, even though nobody asked

Things you did not ask for

Most of these are invisible. Each one protects a partner's first impression or keeps the site legal.

  • Email that lands. SPF, DKIM and DMARC for Microsoft 365, tested before and after.
  • Cookie consent. Required for EU visitors with these tags.
  • Privacy, cookie and legal notice pages. German readers expect a legal notice.
  • Supported server software. For the form handler. The new site itself is static.
  • Security headers. Strict HTTPS and the usual protections.
  • Bing Webmaster Tools and IndexNow. For ChatGPT search and Copilot.
  • Favicon and app icons. The favicon is missing today.
  • A share image for every page and language. All four are broken today.
  • A watchdog. An alert when a page, form or tag breaks.
  • Backup and a 15-minute rollback. Before every upload.
  • One spelling, one board list. "Healthcare", not "Health Care". Titles agreed once.
  • A reply promise for partner enquiries. For example one working day. Needs your yes.
  • Brand assets for the press. Logos and a boilerplate, so partners and journalists quote HCIG correctly.
  • Accessibility to WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard, contrast, captions, reduced motion.
  • Later, not now. A partner portal for insurers to follow their cases, case studies, careers, a chat assistant of our own.

Wording flagged once

Their words stay. These are listed so Irina can decide, nothing more.

  • "100% Of All Recognized Insurance Companies" (services) and "100% Trusted by Global Insurance & Assistance Networks" (home). An insurer cannot verify a 100% claim.
  • Board titles disagree. About says Dr. Amr Abbass is Group Vice Chairman and Group CEO. GHA's news says "chairman and CEO". A hidden block on the services page lists Dr. Ahmed Safwat as Group COO.
  • The name is spelled two ways. "Health Care International Group" in four meta descriptions, "Healthcare International Group" everywhere else.
  • "Medical Tourism Experts in egypt" in the contact page title. A capital letter is an allowed fix.
