# 24/7 Clinic keyword map

Evidence: Google autocomplete, 2026-09-19. 181 searches in English, German,
Polish and Czech, 57 returned suggestions. Raw answers are in
`docs/247clinic-keywords-autocomplete.json`, rebuilt by
`scripts/keywords-247-autocomplete.py`.

Autocomplete shows what people type. It does not show how many. No search
volumes appear here because none were measured. Volumes come from Google Ads
Keyword Planner once an Ads account exists.

## What the evidence says

**Hurghada is where the searches are.** Most smaller places return nothing for
"doctor" in any language. Hurghada returns suggestions in all four.

**People search for a doctor who speaks their language.**

| Language | What Google suggests |
|---|---|
| German | arzt hurghada deutsch, deutsche klinik hurghada, deutscher arzt marsa alam, zahnarzt hurghada deutsch |
| Polish | polski lekarz hurghada |
| Czech | ceska klinika hurghada |

Using this in a title means claiming German, Polish or Czech speaking staff.
That needs confirming by the clinic first.

**Pharmacy searches are strong everywhere.** apotheke, apteka and lékárna
return suggestions for Hurghada, Sahl Hasheesh, Soma Bay, Marsa Alam and
El Quseir. The brief has no pharmacy page. Worth asking whether the clinics
dispense medication and whether to target it.

**Dentist searches are strong in Hurghada.** zahnarzt hurghada preise,
zahnarzt hurghada deutsch, emergency dentist hurghada.

**Hospital terms belong to MedPark.** szpital hurghada, hospital el quseir,
hospital sahl hasheesh, hospital soma bay. 24/7 does not target these.

**Hotel names are searched with a doctor term.** steigenberger ras soma arzt
returned. Nothing returned for Premier Le Rêve or Amwaj with a doctor term.
