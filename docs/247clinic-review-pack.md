## What research did we make?

| Research | What it found |
|---|---|
| Site audit | No canonical, no schema, no Open Graph, no real 404, blog dead since November 2023. |
| Their code | The site already has German and Polish built in, switched off. Turning them on is mostly content. |
| Their database | 30 clinics listed, their site says 28. Le Rêve's map position is 7 km wrong. Every clinic shares one placeholder WhatsApp number. |
| Their films | 16 films. Only Le Rêve has footage of its own clinic. Two films show patients' faces and need consent. |
| Two briefs compared | One real conflict: the URL for hotel pages. |
| Keywords | Hurghada holds the searches. People search for a doctor in their own language. Pharmacy searches are strong everywhere. |
| Security | Issues found in their hosting and admin. Reported to you directly, not on this page. |

## What are the keyword plans?

Full map: [Keywords](https://hcig-passport.vercel.app/247clinic/website/keywords).

- Hotel pages lead with the area: "Doctor in Sahl Hasheesh, 24/7 Clinic at Premier Le Rêve".
- Destination pages lead with the town: "Doctor in Hurghada, 24/7 Hotel Clinics".
- Hospital searches stay with MedPark, so the two sites do not compete.
- German, Polish and Czech tourists add their language to the search. We can only answer that if the clinics confirm those languages are spoken.
