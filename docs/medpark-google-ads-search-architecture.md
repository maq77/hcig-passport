# MedPark Google Ads Search Campaign Architecture

## Campaign Overview
This document outlines the high intent search campaign architecture for MedPark. It targets tourists in Hurghada and El Quseir who have travel insurance.

## Locations
The campaign covers two specific facilities.
1. MedPark Health Hub
Location: Sahl Hasheesh Road, Hurghada.
2. MedPark Hospital
Location: Al Owina, El Quseir.

## Geo-Fence Settings

### Hurghada Coastal Corridor
Target radius: 25 km around MedPark Health Hub.
Coordinates: 27.1234, 33.8234.

### El Quseir Resort Strip
Target radius: 20 km around MedPark Hospital.
Coordinates: 26.1084, 34.2753.
