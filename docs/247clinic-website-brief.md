# 24/7 CLINIC WEBSITE REPOSITIONING & DEVELOPMENT BRIEF
Website: www.247clinic.net

## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

The current website does not clearly communicate what 24/7 Clinic is and why the service is different from a traditional medical center.
The website should be repositioned around one clear message:
24/7 Clinic operates a network of on-site urgent care clinics within hotels and resorts across Egypt’s key coastal destinations on the Red Sea and Mediterranean, providing international travelers with fast and convenient access to medical care.
The website should immediately communicate that:
Our clinics are located inside hotels and resorts across Egypt’s key coastal destinations
Hotel guests can receive medical treatment without unnecessarily travelling to a hospital.
We work with international travel insurance and assistance companies.
Cashless treatment may be available subject to insurance approval.
Patients can contact our team 24/7 through WhatsApp.
Our clinics can manage a wide range of primary and urgent medical conditions on-site.
If hospital-level care is required, we coordinate the next step.
24/7 Clinic is the first international urgent care network outside the United States accredited by the Urgent Care Association through CAUCQ.
The main website conversion goal is:
Visitor → WhatsApp contact → medical coordination → clinic visit / treatment
The website should therefore be built around easy, immediate access to WhatsApp.

## 2. IMPORTANT DEVELOPMENT PRINCIPLE

We do NOT want a complete redesign.
The objective is to keep as much of the current layout and existing website blocks as possible.
Priority:
Change positioning.
Rewrite existing texts.
Rearrange some existing blocks.
Add only necessary new sections.
Improve conversion.
Improve SEO.
Add additional landing pages where necessary.
Improve mobile experience.
Make WhatsApp the primary communication channel.
Avoid unnecessary rebuilding.

## 28. FAQ PAGE

The existing FAQ should be rewritten.
Recommended questions:
Is there a 24/7 Clinic inside my hotel?
Use the Find a Clinic page or contact us on WhatsApp and send your hotel name.
Do you accept travel insurance?
We work with international travel insurers and assistance companies. Coverage depends on the patient’s policy.
Can my treatment be cashless?
Cashless treatment may be available where approved by the insurer and subject to policy conditions.
Do I need to contact my insurance company first?
Not always. Send us your insurance details and our team can advise you on the next step.
What should I send you on WhatsApp?
Please send your name, hotel, location, brief description of the medical problem and insurance information if available.
Can a doctor come to my hotel room?
Hotel room medical visits may be arranged depending on location, availability and medical condition.
What conditions can you treat at the hotel clinic?
Our clinics can assess and treat many urgent and primary medical conditions, minor injuries and other conditions that do not require hospital-level care.
Can you arrange an ambulance?
Yes. If ambulance transport is medically necessary, our team can coordinate it.
What happens if I need a hospital?
If hospital-level diagnostics, treatment or admission are required, our team can coordinate the appropriate hospital referral.
Can you provide documentation for my insurance?
Yes. Medical reports, invoices and other required documentation can be provided according to the case and insurer requirements.
What if my hotel does not have a 24/7 Clinic?
Contact our coordination team on WhatsApp and we will advise you about the nearest available medical solution.
