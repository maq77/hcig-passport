Buy D, the Allrounder, and B, the Hot‑Country Sprinter.

We compared four WAS 300 ambulances on quality, price, running risk and fit for the Red Sea. D and B score highest, cost about the same together as the most expensive option alone, and both run on the Mercedes Sprinter that Egypt already services.

Why these two win on price and quality

  • Top two scores on seven weighted criteria. D stays in the top two in every weight test.
  • Together €230k to €295k, about the price of one MAN (C). Landed in Egypt about €273k to €350k.
  • One chassis, one service plan. Mercedes-Benz Egypt has a workshop in Hurghada. MAN's Egypt agent started in March 2026, for trucks and buses.
  • Proven in Egypt. WAS built 1,000 WAS 300 for the Egyptian Ambulance Organisation and has a Cairo partner.

Why not A and C

  • A, KTW 4x4. Transport vehicle, not a full emergency ambulance. Heaviest at 5.38 t. Only for desert work. B can be ordered 4x4 for less.
  • C, MAN TGE. Best stretcher, but highest price, Dutch radio and livery, and van service in Egypt unproven.
