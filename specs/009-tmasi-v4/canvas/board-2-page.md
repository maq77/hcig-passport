# Design canvas, board 2: the rest of the home

Built on the hero he picked on board 1. Each section shown twice: desktop frame and phone frame,
the same idea in two forms. Real words only. He picks per section.

| Section | Desktop and phone options |
|---|---|
| About + numbers | the v3 approved stats (30,000+ Cases Handled, 570+ Medical Repatriations, 24/7 Operational Desk, 5 Global Hubs) as a quiet count-up band vs inside the About panel; "Your Care. One Call Away." placement |
| Relax band | photo band vs silent loop band |
| Core Values | four numbered cards vs a single line of four with 3D icons |
| Mission and Vision | two cards with read-more vs a split panel with one open at a time |
| Why Choose | five items with 3D icons: row vs staggered grid; phone: 2 x 3 grid or swipe row |
| Our Services | six groups as image cards that open their items in place vs a tabbed list; phone 2 x 3 |
| Quote | inline form vs form beside a coordination image |
| Global Operational Hubs | five office cards linked to the globe (select a card, the hero globe turns there) vs a flat dotted map built from the same land data |
| Partner logos | one quiet row in full colour, each linked to its post |
| News | three latest posts as cards vs one large plus two small |
| Partner band and footer | "Ready to partner" on a loop or still; footer layout |
| Film | where the Watch button lives (About band, Relax band or its own section) |

**Creative moments (Claude proposes two or three, he keeps any he likes):**
- **C1 live office clocks**: the five office cities with their local time, under the hubs (times are
  data; the city names come from the site's addresses; any heading needs his yes).
- **C2 the network grows as you scroll**: a thin teal line connects section to section, echoing the
  arcs (no scroll hijack).
- **C3 numbers in motion**: the stats count up once in Big Noodle numerals over a slow arc.

**3D icon shoot-out** (research R10): three meanings, each from three sources: (a) generated in one
locked style, (b) free CC0 set recoloured, (c) built in our own scene. He picks the source that feels
most human-made, real-world and meaningful; that source makes the whole set.
