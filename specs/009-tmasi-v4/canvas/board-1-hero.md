# Design canvas, board 1: the hero

One page, everything live and moving (real three.js in the page), desktop and phone frames side by
side. He picks one option per row. Nothing on this board is built into the site until he picks.

| Row | Options shown | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Globe look | **L1 dotted network**: teal #009A9C land dots on near-black, offices glow, arcs light. **L2 realistic Earth**: night-side Earth, teal arcs and pins. **L3 glass**: a glass sphere holding the dotted land, soft refraction. **L4 hologram**: latitude and longitude lines, teal wireframe | each with the same five offices and the same story, so only the look differs |
| 2. Story motion | **M1 story then explore**: Egypt lights, arcs draw out nearest to farthest (UAE, Germany, Spain, USA), then slow turn; drag on desktop, tap on phone. **M2 scroll turns it**: as the hero scrolls away the globe turns to each office (native scroll, no pinning). **M3 look only**: slow turn, arcs pulse | timings shown as numbers under each |
| 3. Desktop layout | **D1 split**, words left, globe right (his lead pick). **D2 globe centred** behind the title. **D3 annotated**: split plus leader lines to office labels | real hero words in every option |
| 4. Phone hero | **P1 globe on top, guided tour**, office cards below that follow the tour | his pick from 2026-10-01; shown in a 390 x 844 frame |
| 5. Hero background | **dark** (near-black and indigo #0F205C, teal light) and **light** (white, teal globe) as a switch | hybrid theme: Claude's recommendation marked |
| 6. Headline type | **Big Noodle Titling** (v3) and **Montserrat Bold** (both allowed by the guideline) | same words |
| 7. References | six sites opened and judged by eye, one line each on what to take | links open in a new tab |

Every option shows its reduced-motion still beside it. The board states the 3D weight of the
chosen look (KB) so the trade-off is visible.
