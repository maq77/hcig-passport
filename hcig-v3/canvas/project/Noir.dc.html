Structured Healthcare Solutions for International Patients and Global Partners

With over 40 years of experience, Healthcare International Group (HCIG) is built to manage the full patient journey across international healthcare markets.

Rather than operating as a traditional healthcare provider, HCIG brings structure to a system that is often fragmented connecting urgent care, hospital treatment, and international medical coordination within one clear model.

Our ecosystem spans on-site medical services in hotels and resorts, advanced hospital care, and direct coordination with insurance and assistance partners.

This approach allows us to ensure:

  • faster access to care
  • clear responsibility across the case
  • seamless patient management from first contact to resolution

Operating across multiple markets, HCIG supports international patients, insurers, and global partners with structured, reliable, and scalable healthcare solutions.

We don’t just provide healthcare. We manage it.

How Our System Works

  1. 01

    Immediate Access & First Response

    Patients receive immediate medical support through on-site clinics, urgent care centers, or coordinated medical access points.

  2. 02

    Case Ownership & Medical Coordination

    Each case is taken under full control with continuous coordination between providers, insurers, and partners.

  3. 03

    Escalation to Advanced Care

    When required, patients are transferred to hospital-level treatment within our network or partner facilities.

  4. 04

    Evacuation & Repatriation

    For critical or complex cases, we organize medical evacuation or repatriation across countries with full logistical and medical coordination.

  5. 05

    Insurance & Claims Management

    We work directly with insurance and assistance companies, ensuring efficient approvals, documentation, and claims handling.

  6. 06

    Follow-Up & Case Closure

    We continue managing the patient journey until full recovery or case resolution ensuring continuity and quality of care.
