## Users

- **Hotel guests in Egypt who fall ill or get hurt on holiday.** English, German and Polish speakers first. Often at night, in pain or worried about a child, on a phone, in a hotel room. Their job: get a doctor to the room quickly, in a language they understand, without paying out of pocket if their insurer covers it.
- **People who refer those guests:** hotel reception and guest relations, tour operators and their reps, travel insurers and assistance companies. Their job: hand the guest to someone reliable who handles the paperwork.

## Product Purpose

A doctor comes to the guest's hotel room, 24 hours a day, across 12 destinations in Egypt. Around that visit: portable diagnostics (X-ray, ultrasound, lab), medication delivery, observation beds, ambulance transfer to a partner hospital when needed, and insurance documentation with cashless billing where the insurer allows. Success is a guest who calls or messages within seconds of landing on the site and trusts what they read.

## Positioning

Care comes to the room, and the coordination comes with it: hotel, insurer, family and hospital handled by one multilingual coordinator. A single clinic or hospital cannot truthfully claim the in-hotel reach across the Red Sea, Sinai, the north coast, Cairo and the Nile Valley.

## Operating Context

- Guests reach the service by phone (+20 120 678 8566), WhatsApp, e-mail (info@medcierge.com, care@medcierge.com), through hotel reception, or through the site's callback and booking forms.
- A patient portal and video consultation exist on the live medcierge.com app (login required). The static site links to them.
- Coverage: Cairo, Alexandria, Hurghada, Sharm El Sheikh, El Gouna, Marsa Alam, Alamein, Marsa Matrouh, Port Ghalib, Dahab, Luxor, Aswan. Some towns list 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM rather than 24/7.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three languages: English, German, Polish. German and Polish copy are translations awaiting native review.
- Light and dark colour modes.
- The emergency action is always red and always reachable without scrolling; WhatsApp is always green. Both are HCIG group rules, fixed across every site.
- The preview is served noindex. Canonicals point at medcierge.com with the same paths.
- Unresolved facts (do not settle them in design): the true partner hotel count (the live site says 15+, 50+ and 150+), the source of the 4.9/5 rating and 98% satisfaction, UCA accreditation status, the "Elite Hospital" locations, per-city phone numbers, ISO 9001 and HIPAA evidence, testimonial sources, the 15-minute response promise. All listed in `docs/medcierge-change-list.md`.

## Brand Commitments

- The user asked for a theme that keeps the spirit of the original navy and gold, or improves on it, and "expresses Medcierge" (medical plus concierge). [stated 2026-09-14]

## Product Principles

1. The fastest path is always to a human: a call or WhatsApp is never more than one tap away, in any language, on any page.
2. Calm beats spectacle for someone in pain; motion and visuals must never slow the emergency path.
3. Every claim is one the company can stand behind today. Unverified figures stay flagged, never amplified.
4. Speak the guest's language properly: real translations with their own URLs, not client-side swaps.
5. The concierge half of the name is real: coordination (hotel, insurer, family) is shown, not only medicine.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
